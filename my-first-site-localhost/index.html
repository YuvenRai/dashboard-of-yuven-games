How to play

Build your score and unlock the DJ recording feature.

  • Click the record to drop a beat and earn points.
  • Keep tapping to build a combo streak, up to 8x.
  • Spend points on upgrades to boost your score.
  • At 200 points, use the microphone button to record and play your mix.

Enter a username and Game Code to create or load a game saved for this browser and site address. Accounts are not shared online.
